energy
The Lennard-Jones pair energy in reduced units (sigma = 1, epsilon = 1): U(r) = 4 (r^-12 - r^-6), zero at r = 1, minimum -1 at r0 = 2^(1/6).
energy_cutoff
Shifted-force pair energy with cutoff rc: V(r) = U(r) - U(rc) - U’(rc) (r - rc) for r < rc, else 0. Continuous at rc with V(rc) = 0.
force
The pair force magnitude F(r) = -dU/dr = 24/r (2 r^-12 - r^-6): positive repels (r < r0), zero at r0 = 2^(1/6), negative attracts (r > r0).
force_cutoff
Shifted-force pair force with cutoff rc: F(r) - F(rc) for r < rc, else 0. Continuous at rc.
