Week 3 Ising Metropolis: susceptibility peaks and finite-size T_c
chi(T) = L^2 * (mean(M^2) - mean(|M|)^2) / T; quadratic vertex of 5 centered points
critical-window runs: window-l32, window-l64

L = 32:
  largest window chi: 20.02 at T = 2.35
  5-point quadratic fit (T, chi): (2.25, 10.56), (2.30, 18.85), (2.35, 20.02), (2.40, 15.48), (2.45, 11.77)
  T_peak = 2.3489 +- 0.0049  (jackknife, 20 blocks)

L = 64:
  largest window chi: 65.61 at T = 2.30
  5-point quadratic fit (T, chi): (2.20, 4.48), (2.25, 33.97), (2.30, 65.61), (2.35, 40.96), (2.40, 19.72)
  T_peak = 2.3083 +- 0.0034  (jackknife, 20 blocks)

T_c = 2 * T_peak(64) - T_peak(32) = 2.2677 +- 0.0084
Onsager exact T_c = 2.2692
