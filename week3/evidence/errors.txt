Week 3 Part 3: error analysis of the Metropolis runs (quantity: |M|)
naive_err   = std(|M|)/sqrt(n)                      (assumes independent sweeps)
blocked_err = std(50 block means, ddof=1)/sqrt(50)     (needs block_len >> tau_int)
ratio       = blocked_err / naive_err
tau_int     = 1/2 + sum rho(t) on |M|, sum stopped once lag > 6 * running tau_int
block_len/tau: how many autocorrelation times one block spans; >> 1 is required
NOTE: the 50-block estimate is NOT automatically final or honest; near T_c,
      where block_len/tau approaches 1, it still underestimates the true error.
